About Me

I started out in Technical & Customer Support, working closely with SaaS products, APIs, and databases. That hands-on troubleshooting experience pulled me toward infrastructure — I wanted to understand and build the systems behind the products I was supporting.


I'm now training as a Cloud/DevOps Engineer, focused on Infrastructure as Code, containerization, CI/CD automation, and configuration management. I like taking a manual, error-prone process and turning it into something reliable and repeatable.

Currently: Building end-to-end pipelines with Terraform, Docker, Ansible, and Jenkins — provisioning AWS infrastructure and automating deployment from code push to live application.


Background: Self-taught SQL, REST APIs, and Linux fundamentals before formal DevOps training, with a strong foundation in customer-facing technical problem solving.
